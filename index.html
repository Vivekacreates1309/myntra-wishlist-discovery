01 — The funnel

Where people actually drop off

Wishlist add
Item saved — often while price-comparing, waiting for a price drop, or unsure about size/pattern. First friction: a "double-click to wishlist" popup interrupts browsing before photos even load.
Revisit
User returns to a wishlist with no filter by size, price, or brand — has to scroll every saved item to find what still matters. The wishlist itself is also reported as slow to open.
Add-to-bag hesitation
The single biggest drop-off point, per Myntra's own cited UX research. Users find what they saved — then stall, most often over price or stock/size availability.
Purchase or drop-off
Ideal path: stock + right size + trust in the price → buys. Non-ideal: item's gone, size unavailable, or price moved since it was saved — user exits instead.

03 — Synthesis

The five patterns that matter

  1. Performance lag when accessing or interacting with the wishlist is a major blocker, causing users to abandon the feature before purchase.
  2. Pricing changes — or the perception of them — after adding an item to the wishlist erodes trust and discourages checkout. This is the most-corroborated pattern in the dataset.
  3. No way to filter a large wishlist by size, price, or brand forces users to scroll through irrelevant saved items, adding friction on every revisit.
  4. A wishlist item cap and the removal of a prior "wishlist category" feature both reduce convenience for frequent shoppers.
  5. Add-to-bag hesitation — driven by price uncertainty and stock issues — is the single biggest drop-off point after users relocate a saved item.

04 — Cross-source agreement

What shows up more than once, independently

Pricing trust is the strongest signal in the dataset — three separate 1★ Play Store reviews and one independent Quora post, posted on different dates by different people, all describe the same thing without prompting: prices appearing to rise right after an item is wishlisted. Whether or not it's mechanically true, it's actively working against the wishlist→purchase goal and is worth testing directly in the upcoming interviews.
